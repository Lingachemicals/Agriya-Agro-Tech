Profile

Agriya Agro Tech, Madurai

Agriya Agro Tech is an associate Company of M/s. Linga Chemicals®, Madurai, which is one of the leading companies in the field of agro chemicals, producing micro-nutrients, bio-controlling agents and other agricultural inputs.

Agriya Agro Tech is an ISO 9001:2015 and Indocert certified Company engaged in manufacturing and marketing a diverse range of products derived from microbial organisms such as bacteria and fungi. We focus on protecting the environment by producing eco-friendly products and thereby contributing to the welfare of the society and mankind. Our objective is to maintain the highest standard of quality as well as provide satisfaction and value addition to all our customers. Our aim is to reach out to the farmers across geographic locations to create a better tomorrow.
